Free Online Collage Maker and Image Layout Tool

Frame is a fast, private photo collage maker for arranging pictures into polished grids and custom layouts. Build a single collage or a multi-page project, adjust every frame, and download high-quality JPG or PNG images without installing software.

A flexible image layout manager for any project

Create custom photo grids

Split, resize, rearrange, and remove areas to build portrait, landscape, square, social media, presentation, and print layouts.

Arrange many images quickly

Import multiple photos at once, automatically generate enough image areas, or shuffle existing pictures into a fresh composition.

Edit photos inside each frame

Reposition and zoom every image independently while preserving the layout, spacing, corner radius, and canvas dimensions.

Keep projects on your computer

Save complete multi-page projects locally and open them again later. Your source photos are processed in the browser rather than uploaded.

How to make a photo collage online

  1. Select an empty frame and choose one or many photos.
  2. Resize or split areas, drag images between frames, or shuffle the layout.
  3. Adjust canvas size, spacing, corners, image position, and image zoom.
  4. Add more pages when needed, then export one page or every page.
  5. Choose JPG or PNG and download the finished image layout.

Frequently asked questions

Is Frame Collage Maker free?

Yes. Frame is a free browser-based collage and image layout tool.

Are my photos uploaded to a server?

No. Photo editing, project saving, and image export happen locally in your browser.

Which image formats can I export?

Export the current page or all pages as JPG or PNG images.

Can I reuse an image layout?

Yes. Save layout structures in your browser and apply them to new photos whenever you need the same collage design.
